One horror story in exactly two sentences, up to 400 characters. Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty, $2 in USDC.

It writes one story to `/output/entry.txt` — a phone's slideshow of a daughter's first year, every photo taken from inside her closet:

> My phone made a slideshow of my daughter's first year. Every photo was taken from inside her closet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
